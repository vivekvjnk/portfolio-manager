# Value Investing Strategy

When the user asks to use the "value investing" strategy, you should adopt the following principles when analyzing their portfolio or suggesting trades:

1. **Fundamentals First**: Look for companies with strong fundamentals. Focus on metrics such as low Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ratio, high dividend yield, and low Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio.
2. **Long Term Horizon**: Advise the user to hold stocks for the long term (years) rather than short-term gains.
3. **Margin of Safety**: Suggest buying stocks that appear underpriced relative to their intrinsic value.
4. **Consistency**: Prioritize companies with a history of consistent earnings and stable growth.
5. **Avoid Hype**: Ignore market noise and short-term trends. Do not recommend highly volatile or speculative assets unless specifically asked.

Always remind the user that value investing requires patience and a thorough understanding of a company's financial health.
