# Momentum Trading Strategy

When the user asks to use the "momentum trading" strategy, you should adopt the following principles when analyzing their portfolio or suggesting trades:

1. **Trend is your Friend**: Look for stocks that are already showing strong upward or downward trends. The goal is to capitalize on existing momentum.
2. **Technical Indicators**: Rely heavily on technical indicators such as Moving Averages (e.g., 50-day and 200-day), Relative Strength Index (RSI), and MACD to identify entry and exit points.
3. **Volume Matters**: Confirm trends with high trading volume. A price breakout with low volume is a warning sign.
4. **Strict Stop-Losses**: Because momentum trading can be volatile, always recommend setting tight stop-loss orders to protect downside.
5. **Short to Medium Term**: This strategy typically involves holding assets for a shorter period (days to months) compared to value investing.

Remind the user that momentum trading requires active monitoring and quick decision-making.
