# Portfolio Benchmarks & Risk Threshold Specification

## 1. Sector Concentration Thresholds
To prevent catastrophic portfolio drawdowns during sector-specific corrections, the following allocation caps are enforced:

| Sector / Category | Current Level (Baseline) | Target Allocation Cap | Action Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Power & Energy** | ~53.5% | **≤ 30.0%** | Sell underperforming power holdings on pullbacks; redeploy into defensive / growth sectors. |
| **PSUs (Public Sector Undertakings)** | ~59.3% | **≤ 30.0%** | Restrict new PSU purchases; trim positions with weak relative strength. |
| **Defence & Shipbuilding** | ~21.8% | **≤ 20.0%** | Hold core gainers (HAL, BEL); lock profits on extended moves. |
| **Financials** | ~8.0% | **15.0% – 25.0%** | Accumulate quality private/large PSU banks on confirmed technical demand zones. |
| **Retail / Consumer / Tech** | ~10.7% | **15.0% – 25.0%** | Diversify into non-cyclical, high-growth leaders. |
| **War Chest Reserve (Cash)** | Variable | **≥ 15.0% – 20.0%** | Maintain liquid reserve for deployment during confirmed market regime transitions. |

## 2. Underperformer Review Criteria
Positions meeting any of the following criteria must be flagged for liquidation or rebalancing:
1. **Relative Weakness**: Negative 5-day return when Nifty 50 is positive by > 1.0%.
2. **Structure Breakdown**: Close below 50-day EMA on high volume (> 1.5x average).
3. **Stagnant Demand**: Price trading in a narrow zone for > 10 trading days without higher high / higher low progression.
4. **Earnings Exclusion**: Upcoming earnings report within 5 trading days.

## 3. Benchmark Relative Return Score
- **Outperforming Nifty 50**: Portfolio Return - Nifty Return > +0.25% → Maintain current exposure.
- **In-Line with Nifty 50**: Difference within [-0.25%, +0.25%] → Selective setup execution only.
- **Underperforming Nifty 50**: Difference < -0.25% → Freeze new momentum entries; initiate risk audit and sector trim.
